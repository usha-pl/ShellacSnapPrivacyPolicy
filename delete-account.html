Delete Your Account & Data

Shellac Snap does not create its own user accounts. It connects to your existing Discogs.com account using a Personal Access Token, which you generate and control entirely through your Discogs settings.

What data exists, and where

All of your personal data — your Discogs token, scan history, and spreadsheet entries — is stored locally on your own device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of this data immediately; nothing remains on our servers.

The only data retained on our server is an anonymized daily scan count tied to your Discogs user ID, used solely to enforce a fair usage limit. This entry is automatically deleted within 24–48 hours regardless of any request.
